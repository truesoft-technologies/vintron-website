About the Company ↘

Your licensed electrical partner in the UAE

Linu Tech Contracting is a licensed electrical contracting company operating under the Vintron Group of Companies. We serve commercial and industrial clients across Sharjah and the UAE, backed by the group’s 22+ years of reputation for quality, integrity and on-time delivery.

Our team of qualified engineers and certified technicians specialises in complete electrical installations, MEP project coordination and ongoing maintenance — delivering every project to the highest UAE safety and compliance standards.

➜ Electrical Contracting — licensed installations for commercial and industrial sites

What We Do ↘

Our Services

Complete electrical and MEP contracting solutions for commercial and industrial projects across the UAE.

Electrical Contracting

Complete electrical installations for commercial and industrial projects, from design and supply to testing and commissioning.

MEP Services

Mechanical, electrical and plumbing integrated project delivery — coordinated execution from a single experienced team.

Power Distribution

LV/MV switchgear, distribution boards, busbar systems and cabling infrastructure designed for reliability and efficiency.

Lighting Systems

Interior and exterior lighting design and installation — from energy-efficient LED systems to architectural and industrial lighting.

Safety & Fire Alarm

Fire alarm systems, emergency lighting, earthing and bonding solutions installed to UAE Civil Defence and SEWA compliance standards.

Maintenance Contracts

Ongoing electrical maintenance, periodic inspection and testing services to keep your facilities safe and code-compliant year-round.
