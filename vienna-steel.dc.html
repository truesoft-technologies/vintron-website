About the Company

Precision engineering for UAE industry

Founded in Sharjah and operating as part of the Vintron Group of Companies, Vienna Turning Eng. & Steel has built a strong reputation serving the construction and industrial sectors across the UAE. With more than 22 years of group heritage behind every project, we bring proven reliability to every contract.

Our workshop capabilities span the full steel fabrication lifecycle — from design interpretation and raw material selection through to precision CNC turning, milling, structural assembly and surface finishing — giving clients a single, accountable partner from drawing to delivery.
