About the Company ↘

Expert auto care for UAE conditions

Venus Auto Maintenance is the automotive services arm of Vintron Group of Companies, operating a full-service workshop in Sharjah, UAE. We specialise in vehicle maintenance, mechanical repair, electrical diagnostics and preventative servicing tailored to the demands of UAE roads and extreme summer conditions.

Serving both fleet operators and individual vehicle owners, our experienced team of certified technicians provides complete car care with genuine parts, transparent pricing and a commitment to keeping every vehicle road-ready across Sharjah and the wider UAE.

➜ Full-Service Maintenance — complete vehicle care under one roof
➜ Fleet Management — dedicated programmes for corporate & commercial fleets
➜ Sharjah-Based Workshop — convenient location serving the UAE

What We Offer ↘

Our Services

Periodic Maintenance

Scheduled servicing, oil and fluid changes, filter replacements and manufacturer-recommended checks to keep your vehicle performing at its best.

Engine Diagnostics

Computerised engine diagnostics using the latest scanning tools to accurately identify fault codes, sensor issues and performance problems for fast rectification.

AC & Cooling Systems

Air conditioning service, regas, leak detection and full repair — essential maintenance for UAE summer conditions and extreme heat.

Tyre & Wheel Services

Tyre fitting, balancing, computerised wheel alignment and rotation to maximise tyre life, fuel efficiency and safe handling on UAE roads.

Brake & Suspension

Comprehensive brake inspection, pad and disc replacement, suspension checks and absorber servicing to ensure safety and ride quality.

Fleet Management

Dedicated maintenance programmes for corporate and commercial fleets — scheduled servicing, priority turnaround and fleet health reporting to minimise downtime.
