About the Company ↘

Your trusted supply partner across the UAE

Vintron General Trading LLC is the founding pillar of the Vintron Group of Companies, established in 2002 in Sharjah, UAE. As the group's original business and trading backbone, it has spent over two decades building a reputation for reliability, quality product sourcing and fast fulfilment.

Operating across the UAE and the wider GCC, the company supplies a comprehensive range of products — from electrical accessories and plumbing materials to safety equipment, educational trainers and auto maintenance products — to contractors, institutions and businesses of all sizes.

➜ Electrical Accessories — switches, sockets, MCBs, distribution boards
➜ Safety Equipment — PPE, helmets, harnesses, fire safety, first aid
➜ Plumbing Materials — pipes, fittings, valves, water systems
